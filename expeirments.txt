(flow_fields.py)

The general idea is: TrajGRU works by tracking sequential moving patterns 
that are relevant to the target variable. Why not find the correlation 
between what the flow field vectors point to, and some meaningful feature?

The `flows` tuple returned contains several flow trajectories generated
during inference — flow 1 is generated at the firstmost layer and contains 
the most atmospheric relevant trajectories.

Several links (5) are contained within the flow tensor and, themselves, 
contain two channels — one for the x-component of the traced neighbor,
and the other for the y-component.

The x/y components of each link is then averaged, creating a mean resultant
vector — the learned trajectory. 

Usage:
  - Flow Field Animation overlay of 
  - Aligmnet score between meaningful features (z_1000)

The aligmnet (consine similarity) score: Each trajectory (or link) is trained to determine the 
x/y components of a vector that identifies a pattern in motion; and it 
does this for every pixel observed (H,W). This means that the trajectory
tensors have a size = (2, H, W).The tensors are then stacked along the first 
dimensions (0), and then averaged; producing a single vector that generalizes 
the path observed by a single window (u_agg and v_agg). These vectors are then
compared to the direction of movement of a (particular) variable — computed using
np.gradient.
